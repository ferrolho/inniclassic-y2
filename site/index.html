MENU

The iPod look

It looks like an iPod. It moves like one.

The Classic themes use the measurements of the iPod classic: a 30 px status bar, 38 px rows with a drawn chevron, and the menu shadow over the album art.

  • The art next to the main menu and the Music menu pans and zooms slowly, as on the iPod.
  • Menus slide in from the right and back to the left. Into Cover Flow, the two panes move apart.
  • Classic Light, Classic Dark, or Auto: light by day, dark after sunset. A theme change applies at once, and the music keeps playing.

Cover Flow

Spin through your albums.

Cover Flow has the positions, reflections and order of the iPod: by artist, then album, without a leading “The”. A fast spin does not drop frames.

  • The text under each cover shows the album artist, or “Various Artists” for a compilation.
  • Covers come from the tags or from cover.jpg / folder.jpg.

Letter jump

4,000 songs? Turn faster.

Turn the wheel fast and the list goes letter by letter, with a large letter box, as on the iPod. It works in all the alphabetical lists and in Cover Flow.

Now Playing

The screen you look at most.

An angled cover with a reflection, a glass progress bar and iPod times: 2:08, -0:34, 1:02:08.

  • Hold Next or Previous to seek, from 4× up to 120×. Hold Play/Pause to stop.
  • Hold the centre button for the song menu: Add to Playlist, On-The-Go, Favorites, Browse Album, Browse Artist.
  • Spectrum shows the music in the colour of the cover. Total Time shows the length in place of the time left.
  • Star ratings, favorites, and the smart playlists Recently Played and Top 25 Most Played.

Lyrics

Sing along. In any script.

One push of the centre button shows the lyrics over the full screen. Synced lyrics follow the song, with soft fades at the top and bottom.

  • From .lrc files next to the song, or from the tags (MP3 USLT, FLAC, ALAC).
  • Chinese or Japanese? Add a .romanised.lrc file and choose Original, Romanised (pinyin, romaji) or both lines.
  • The screen stays on while the lyrics show and the music plays.

Your library

All your music, the iPod way.

Artists, Albums, Compilations, Songs, Genres, Composers, Playlists and Folders. Hide or reorder the menu items you do not use. Shuffle Songs plays the whole library.

  • MP3, FLAC, WAV, OGG Vorbis, Opus, ALAC and AAC, on the internal storage and the microSD card.
  • Each play past the half (or 4 minutes) counts: for Top 25, and for Last.fm in .scrobbler.log.
  • Make the library scan cache on your computer, so a large card is ready in seconds.

Play On

Send the song to the speaker.

Main menu → Play On finds the Sonos, DLNA and Google Cast speakers on your Wi-Fi. Choose one, and the music plays there. No phone, no Google Play Services.

  • The wheel stays in control: next track, pause, seek and volume go to the speaker.
  • On a Sonos, the app shows the title, artist, album, progress and cover.
  • The speaker icon takes the place of the Wi-Fi icon, and Now Playing shows the speaker name.

The status bar, 3× larger: the speaker icon, Bluetooth, play state, battery and clock.

And more

More than an iPod.

The Y2 has Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and an FM tuner. uses all three, from the wheel.

Bluetooth and cars

Pair headphones or a car with the wheel only. The car shows title and artist, and its buttons work with the screen off. The music pauses when they disconnect and plays on when they come back.

FM radio

The internal tuner, with presets and a scan. It plays on with the screen off.

Podcasts

Subscribe to feeds, stream an episode, or download it for later.

Audiobooks

Books and chapters, with a bookmark that is kept for each book.

Jellyfin and Wi-Fi transfer

Sync music from your Jellyfin server (QuickConnect), or copy files from a browser to the built-in web server.

Even volume steps

30 equal steps of 2 dB, kept for each output. Equalizer and effects run only when you turn them on.

Settings and buttons

Small things, done right.

  • Settings in iPod groups (Playback, Display, Now Playing, Status Bar…), with On/Off switches. Export and import all settings as one file.
  • Each push does one action, on release. One vibration for each push and wheel step, and the click has its own volume.
  • No Android pop-ups: questions, the Power screen (hold the Power button) and pairing codes are normal list screens that the wheel can use.
  • A “Battery low” banner at 10 % and an iPod Please charge screen at 2 %.
  • Time zone, 12 or 24 hour clock, and the time from the internet.

Install

Try it on your Y2.

Two ways: flash the full firmware, or install the app on a Y2 that already runs InniClassic.

Firmware (recommended)

  1. Download rom_y2.zip from Releases.
  2. Open the Innioasis Updater and choose the zip as the local firmware package.
  3. Follow the steps. When the Y2 restarts, it starts in the Main menu.

A full flash erases the internal storage. Music on the microSD card stays. Keep the cable in until the update is done.

App only (APK)

The firmware has ADB on. Connect the Y2 by USB:

adb uninstall com.themoon.y1
adb install InniClassic-<version>.apk

Uninstall first only if the new APK has a different signing key. Your music and playlists on the card stay.

Build it yourself

Android Studio or JDK 17 with the Android SDK:

git clone 
cd 
./gradlew assembleDebug
